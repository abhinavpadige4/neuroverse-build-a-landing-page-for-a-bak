Our Story

A bakery built on patience, flour, and community.

Crumb Culture began in 2014 with a single stone hearth oven and a stubborn belief: great bread can't be rushed. A decade later, we still ferment our sourdough for 36 hours, still mill our rye in-house, and still greet every regular by name.

We source organic flour from small Pacific Northwest mills, partner with local farms for our butter and eggs, and bake everything from scratch — no shortcuts, no preservatives, no compromises.
